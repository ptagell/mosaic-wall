Look & atmosphere

layered over everything the wall shows, in this order: colour → light → overlay
Colour
a look colour-grades everything — photos and art alike; tint washes the wall in a single amber tone
Light
vignette darkens the edges; night dim softens brightness through the evening by each tile's clock (independent of the schedule below)
Ambience
a particle field (snow, bokeh, aurora…) drawn over whatever is showing — one continuous field across every tile; density scales how much of it there is; spotlight pulses one tile at a time around the wall

Good to know

On the tiles · tap any tile to peek at its link status (green dot) and the photo's date & place — or the artwork's name
· tiles reconnect on their own after wifi blips and quietly reload every ~6 hours to stay fresh
· during scheduled sleep the screens go black and stop rendering; they wake with a fresh reload
Picking photos · no two tiles ever show the same photo at the same time
· recently shown photos rest before they can reappear — within each scene's own pool
· small pools (one person with few photos) rotate through everything before repeating
Staying current · photo pools refresh every hour, so new Immich uploads join without touching this page
· “On this day” renews itself at midnight
· every setting here is saved instantly and survives restarts
